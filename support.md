## What Lucky Fill Light Does

Lucky Fill Light turns your screen into a soft fill light for photos and videos. You can adjust color, brightness, and saturation, use built-in presets, unlock a full color wheel (in-app purchase), and use camera mode for preview, photos, and recording.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### The screen does not stay bright enough

Open **Settings** in the App and enable **Auto max brightness on launch**. You can also raise brightness with the slider on the control panel. Check that **Low Power Mode** is off in iOS Settings.

### Camera or microphone permission was denied

Go to **iOS Settings → Lucky Fill Light** and enable **Camera** and **Microphone** (microphone is only needed for video recording). Then reopen the App.

### I cannot save photos or videos

Enable **Photos** access for Lucky Fill Light in **iOS Settings → Lucky Fill Light**. The App saves captures to your photo library on your device.

### Color wheel is locked

The free color wheel is unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase. Tap the locked color wheel and complete the purchase through the App Store. If you already purchased, open **Settings** in the App and tap **Restore Purchases**.

### Purchase did not unlock the color wheel

1. Confirm you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase.  
2. In App **Settings**, tap **Restore Purchases**.  
3. Restart the App.  
4. If the issue continues, email us with your purchase receipt from Apple.

### App language

Open **Settings** in the App and choose a language under **Language**. The App interface will update to your selection.

### Leaving camera mode

In camera mode, tap the **✕** button at the top-left of the preview to return to the main fill-light screen. This does not quit the App.
