# Privacy Policy — Lucky Fill Light

**Last updated:** May 26, 2026  
**App:** Lucky Fill Light  
**Bundle ID:** `com.buguang.app`

This Privacy Policy describes how Lucky Fill Light (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use our iOS application. If you have questions, contact us at the email listed in the [Contact](#contact) section below.

---

## Summary

Lucky Fill Light is a screen-based fill-light and camera utility app. **We do not operate our own servers to collect your personal data.** Most information stays on your device. Apple processes in-app purchases. Photos and videos you save go to your device’s photo library under your control.

---

## Information We Do Not Collect

We do **not**:

- Require an account or login  
- Collect your name, email, or phone number through the App  
- Run third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the App  
- Sell your personal information  

---

## Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores preferences locally on your iPhone or iPad using Apple’s standard storage (UserDefaults and SwiftData), including:

- Fill-light color settings (hue, saturation, brightness)  
- Selected preset and custom preset names and colors you create  
- App settings (language preference, auto brightness, keep screen awake, restore brightness on exit)  
- Whether the color wheel in-app purchase has been unlocked on this device  

This data remains on your device unless you delete the App or reset your device. We do not sync it to our servers.

---

## Device Permissions and How We Use Them

### Camera

Used when you open **Camera mode** to show a live preview and to take photos. Camera data is processed on your device for preview and capture. We do not upload your camera feed to our servers.

### Microphone

Used only when you **record video** in Camera mode to include audio in the video file. We do not upload microphone audio to our servers.

### Photo Library — Read

Used when you choose a **reference image** from your library to sample colors for the fill light. The App reads the image you select on your device. We do not upload that image to our servers.

### Photo Library — Write

Used when you **save a photo or video** you capture in the App. Saved media is written to your photo library using Apple’s system APIs. We do not keep a separate copy on our servers.

You can deny or revoke any permission in **Settings → Lucky Fill Light** on your device. Some features will not work without the related permission.

---

## In-App Purchases

The App offers a **one-time, non-consumable** purchase to unlock the free color wheel (product ID: `luckycolor`).  

Payment and purchase records are handled by **Apple** through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide us with limited purchase-related information according to Apple’s policies (for example, to validate that a purchase occurred).

You can restore purchases from **Settings** inside the App where “Restore Purchases” is available.

---

## Screen Brightness

The App can temporarily adjust **system screen brightness** while you use it (for example, maximum brightness for fill light). If enabled, brightness may be restored when you leave the App, depending on your settings.
