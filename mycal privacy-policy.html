Privacy Policy

Secure Vault - Local
Last updated: June 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Secure Vault - Local (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use our iOS application. We designed the App with privacy in mind: your photos and videos stay on your device and are protected with encryption.

1. Overview

Secure Vault - Local provides encrypted local storage for photos and videos. Unlock is protected by Face ID, Touch ID, or a PIN you set. We do not operate user accounts, cloud storage, or advertising networks. We do not sell your personal data.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your iPhone or iPad only:

  • Encrypted media files — Photos and videos you choose to import, encrypted with AES-256-GCM before being saved in the App’s private storage.
  • Encrypted thumbnails — Small preview images derived from your media, also encrypted.
  • Metadata — Non-sensitive information such as import date and display labels (metadata does not contain the image or video content itself).
  • Unlock PIN — Stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • Encryption key — A random AES key generated on first use and stored in the iOS Keychain. It never leaves your device.
  • App preferences — Such as your selected language and premium purchase status (stored locally).

None of this data is transmitted to our servers because we do not operate backend servers for the App.

3. Photo Library Access

The App requests access to your Photo Library only when you choose to:

  • Import — Select photos or videos to encrypt and store in the vault.
  • Save — Export a decrypted photo or video back to your Photo Library when you tap the download/save button.

We do not access your Photo Library in the background. We do not upload your library contents anywhere. Importing creates an encrypted copy inside the App; your original files in the system Photos app are not modified or deleted by the App.

4. Face ID / Touch ID

If you use biometric unlock, authentication is handled entirely by iOS. We do not receive or store your biometric data.

5. Purchases

The App offers an optional one-time in-app purchase (Unlimited Album Unlock) to unlock unlimited storage. Payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide us with purchase confirmation so the App can enable premium features on your device. Please refer to Apple’s Privacy Policy for how Apple handles payment data.

6. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, track, or share:

  • Name, email address, or contact details (unless you email us for support)
  • Location data
  • Analytics or advertising identifiers
  • Contents of your encrypted vault
  • Your unlock PIN or biometric data

The App does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or social media integrations.

7. Security

Media in the vault is encrypted using industry-standard AES-256-GCM. Keys and PINs are stored in the iOS Keychain. The App locks automatically when it goes to the background, returning you to the lock screen.

Important: If you forget your unlock PIN, we cannot recover your encrypted data. There is no PIN reset or cloud backup of vault contents provided by us.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your vault data remains on your device until you delete items inside the App or uninstall the App. Deleting the App removes its local storage and Keychain entries associated with the App from your device, subject to iOS behavior.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International Users

Because all processing occurs on your device, your data is not transferred to us or to countries outside your control via our systems.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
