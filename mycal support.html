Getting Started

How do I open the private vault?

  1. Open Lucky Calculator — it looks and works like a normal calculator.
  2. Enter your unlock password using the number keys (default password: 123456).
  3. Press = (equals).
  4. If the password is correct, you will enter your private photo and video library.

How do I change my password?

  1. Unlock the vault and tap the gear icon (Settings) at the top right.
  2. Enter a new password (4–8 digits) and confirm it.
  3. Tap Save Password.
  4. After you lock the App, use the new password to unlock.

How do I lock the App?

  • Open Settings (gear icon) and tap Lock at the bottom, or
  • Switch to another app or press the Home button — the App locks automatically when it goes to the background.

Photos & Videos

How do I import photos or videos?

Tap the + button at the top right of the vault, then select items from your Photo Library. They are encrypted and stored inside the App.

How do I save an item back to my Photo Library?

Open the item, then tap the download icon at the bottom left. The decrypted file is saved to your system Photos app.

How do I delete items?

Tap Select, choose one or more items, then tap Delete. You can also delete a single item from its full-screen preview using the trash icon.

What file types are supported?

Common photo formats (JPEG, HEIC, PNG, etc.) and video formats (MOV, MP4, etc.) from your Photo Library. Files are encrypted as-is without re-encoding.

Free Plan & Purchases

How many items can I store for free?

The free version allows up to 10 items (photos and videos combined).

How do I unlock unlimited storage?

When you reach the free limit, follow the in-app prompt to purchase a one-time upgrade for unlimited storage. Payment is handled securely by Apple.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings → Restore Purchases, or use the restore option on the upgrade screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Language

Lucky Calculator supports English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, German, and Arabic. Change the language in Settings → Language.

Security & Privacy

  • All vault media is encrypted with AES-256-GCM on your device.
  • We do not upload your photos, videos, or password to any server.
  • If you forget your password, encrypted data cannot be recovered. There is no reset option by design.
