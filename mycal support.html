Getting Started

How do I unlock the vault?

  1. Open Secure Vault - Local — you will see the lock screen.
  2. Tap Unlock with Face ID or Touch ID (on supported devices), or tap Use PIN Instead.
  3. Enter your PIN on the number pad (default PIN: 123456).
  4. When authentication succeeds, you enter your encrypted photo and video library.

How do I change my PIN?

  1. Unlock the vault and tap the gear icon (Settings) at the top right.
  2. Enter a new PIN (4–8 digits) and confirm it.
  3. Tap Save PIN.
  4. After you lock the App, use the new PIN to unlock.

How do I lock the App?

  • Open Settings (gear icon) and tap Lock at the bottom, or
  • Switch to another app — the App locks automatically when it goes to the background.

Photos & Videos

How do I import photos or videos?

Tap the + button at the top right of the vault, then select items from your Photo Library. They are encrypted and stored locally inside the App.

How do I save an item back to my Photo Library?

Open the item, then tap the download icon at the bottom left. The decrypted file is saved to your system Photos app.

How do I delete items?

Tap Select, choose one or more items, then tap Delete. You can also delete a single item from its full-screen preview using the trash icon.

What file types are supported?

Common photo formats (JPEG, HEIC, PNG, etc.) and video formats (MOV, MP4, etc.) from your Photo Library. Files are encrypted as-is without re-encoding.

Free Plan & Purchases

How many items can I store for free?

The free version allows up to 10 items (photos and videos combined).

How do I unlock unlimited storage?

Go to Settings → Upgrade to Unlimited Storage, or tap the star icon at the top of the vault. You can also follow the in-app prompt when you reach the free limit. Purchase is a one-time upgrade (Unlimited Album Unlock) handled securely by Apple.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings → Restore Purchases, or use the restore option on the upgrade screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Language

Secure Vault - Local supports English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, German, and Arabic. Change the language in Settings → Language.

Security & Privacy

  • All vault media is encrypted with AES-256-GCM on your device.
  • We do not upload your photos, videos, or PIN to any server.
  • If you forget your PIN, encrypted data cannot be recovered. There is no reset option by design.
